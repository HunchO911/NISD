    How the simulation works

    A tariff rise of dt points changes the landed price by dt / (100 + t₀). Import demand falls by |elasticity| times that change, half in year 1 and fully afterwards. The subsidy, converted to USD, buys annual domestic output at the stated output ratio, phased in at 20%, 50%, 80% and 100% over years 1 to 4. Each USD of new output displaces one USD of imports, and total displacement never exceeds the product's imports. Jobs are direct (output displaced × employment multiplier) plus indirect at 1.5 × direct.
